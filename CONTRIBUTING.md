# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in AI Act Readiness GRC MVP.

## Ways to contribute

- Report bugs or unexpected behavior.
- Suggest usability improvements or GRC workflows.
- Improve documentation.
- Identify regulatory references that need review.

## Before submitting a change

1. Describe the problem or proposed improvement.
2. Explain the expected behavior.
3. Cite official sources for regulatory suggestions.
4. Do not include personal, confidential or sensitive information.
5. Test changes in a modern browser.

Regulatory contributions do not automatically constitute legal validation or endorsement of the project. For security issues, follow `SECURITY.md`.
